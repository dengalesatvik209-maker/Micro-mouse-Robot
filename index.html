Micromouse Robot

An autonomous maze-solving robot designed to navigate a maze without human control — sensing surroundings, building a map, planning a route, and moving with repeatable precision.

01 Sense 02 Map 03 Plan 04 Move

What is a Micromouse?

  • Micromouse is a small autonomous robot challenge.
  • The robot detects maze walls using onboard distance sensors.
  • It builds an internal map and decides where to move.
  • Its goal is to reach the maze centre or target cell.
  • A faster speed run may be attempted after learning the maze.
Core Idea

Sense surroundings, update the map, choose a route, and move accurately.

04 / Hardware

Main Hardware Components

Core subsystems from our prototype build and standard Micromouse architecture.

Controller

Arduino Uno in our prototype (supports Arduino, ESP32, STM32, or other microcontrollers).

Drive System

Two DC geared BO motors, high-grip wheels, and an L298N dual H-bridge motor driver.

Sensors

HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor in our prototype; extensible to IR/ToF sensors, encoders, and IMU.

Power

4× AA battery pack, voltage regulation, rocker power switch, and colour-coded jumper wiring.

Chassis

Laser-cut 3 mm chassis body, upper electronics deck, and front sensor mount designed in CAD.

Optional Modules

SG90 servo mount for scanning, status LEDs, buzzer, display, or gyroscope.

05 / Sensing

Sensors and Their Functions

Sensor feedback helps the robot understand the maze and control its motion.

Distance Sensors (Ultrasonic / IR / ToF)

Detect nearby walls and estimate obstacle distance.

  • Front sensing: identifies a wall ahead before turning.
  • Side sensing: maps left and right walls in corridors.

Wheel Encoders

Estimate wheel rotation and exact travel distance.

  • Measures single-cell forward displacement.
  • Supports repeatable straight runs and turns.

Gyroscope / IMU

Track rotation and maintain straight heading.

  • Reduces drift over long straight corridors.
  • Improves 90° and 180° turn accuracy.

10 / Algorithm

Maze Algorithm: Flood Fill

A common Micromouse method for finding the shortest route through maze cells.

Algorithm Steps

  1. Assign the goal cell a distance of zero (0).
  2. Spread distance values outward through open cells.
  3. Walls block distance propagation between adjacent cells.
  4. Move to a neighbouring cell with a lower distance value.
  5. Recalculate distances whenever new walls change the map.
